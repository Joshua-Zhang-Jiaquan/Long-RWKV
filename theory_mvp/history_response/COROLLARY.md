# Response-based sufficient condition

This algebraic consequence was added during follow-up preparation. It is supplementary to the already frozen primary paired-CE contrast, not an additional preregistered inferential endpoint.

For each fixed problem, the frozen exact identity gives

    E_second_original + E_second_flipped_target = 8 * mean_pair_CE.

Correct cross-entropies are nonnegative. Therefore

    E_info = E_first + E_second_original
           <= E_first + 8 * mean_pair_CE.

If the right side is below 4 ln(2), the information-set two-pass model beats every one-call product predictor on this target law. The statement also holds after taking an expectation over a declared problem/position law. It is sufficient, not necessary, and is a quality criterion: the separate previously measured latency conditions are still needed for a quality–cost statement.

The coefficient eight cannot be improved without more assumptions on the two prediction laws: an arbitrary predictor can assign arbitrarily small error to the flipped targets while retaining nonzero original error. In that limit the inequality approaches equality.

The mean response diagnostic equals mean response floor plus mean centering penalty. Both terms matter. Response strength alone supplies no upper bound on error. No population or natural-language guarantee follows from the empirical enumerated-panel certificate.

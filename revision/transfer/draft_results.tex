\section{Results and limits of inference}
\paragraph{Prospective test: outcomes pending.}
The six fixed-budget training pipelines have been launched after source-only
runtime and numerical qualification. The source/test class split, calibration
estimator, fallback, panels, estimands, success criteria and analysis source are
frozen. All six prediction artifacts must be sealed before any held-out neural
evaluation. This paragraph will be replaced by the complete lineage and
held-out results. Training loss, runtime qualification, and algebraic feasibility
are not evidence that the proposed predictor transfers.

\paragraph{Completed evidence motivating the test.}
In the earlier paired-parity study, three near/balanced adaptation pairs share
one selected competent starting checkpoint. At fixed16K training length,
balanced positions reduce far conditional error by 6.0506 nats, with a
problem-bootstrap interval $[5.3916,6.7353]$ conditional on those models.
All three pairs favor balanced training. A fresh header/task layout panel
retains a 7.6266-nat reduction $[6.9081,8.3015]$ with nearby instructions and
distant task information. These interventions support evidence use under the
declared layouts. They do not establish reliable full-pipeline learning or
transfer to a different dependency class.

The exhaustive response audit retains three collateral errors among55,296
unaffected comparisons. Regrouping distractors changes one model's mean error
by4.09 nats. The earlier short-training campaign succeeds only for seed71, and
1K-to-16K adaptation and changed-interface probes fail. These results constrain
the mechanism interpretation and are retained in the supporting experiments.

\paragraph{Quality and cost answer different questions.}
The earlier equal-call gain agrees with $4\log2$ to within
$1.3\times10^{-4}$ nats when both learned errors are small. It does not
independently forecast the error of an unseen policy or workload. The measured
1.30-second recurrent two-call versus2.00-second attention two-call execution
supports a restricted1.5-second mean-budget certificate under its FP32/IEEE
implementation. It does not establish an optimized practical frontier or a
memory advantage. Cached causal decoding and other output factorizations remain
outside that certificate (Appendix~\ref{app:budget-certificate}).

\paragraph{Scope.}
The new task still has eight binary outputs,16 valid endpoints, explicit public
equations and synthetic distractors. Success would demonstrate prospective
selection across the declared code classes and fresh task-training lineages,
not open-ended language generation. All lineages share the same public pretrained
backbone. Full context is retained between calls, so the mechanism does not
recover information discarded by a state-only memory. Numerical checks use the
FLA implementation; parity with official RWKV kernels remains unverified.

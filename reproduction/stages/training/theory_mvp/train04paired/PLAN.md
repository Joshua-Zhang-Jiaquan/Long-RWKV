# Paired policy-history development experiment

Motivation: all six completed terminal development arms fail the original N8 parity task and barely respond to revealed partner values. Explicit public slot labels improve some copying outcomes but not parity. The next experiment changes training-history coupling directly, without changing the public equations, target law, model architecture, held-out structures, or inference policy.

Both arms start from the completed label-mixture step1500 checkpoint d503a96ed8152842a2f56b35911c11732f62f316d9b5483e4f4599aab263425f, including optimizer state. This is a common continuation point, not a successful recipe or a confirmation-selected model. Both use policy-history supervision only, uniform over the two information-set rounds, and exact marginal soft targets. Thus their common population excess risk is E_information_set/N. Comparison with earlier mixed-objective runs alone cannot isolate the effect of pairing.

For each public condition, draw two histories. The independent control draws both free-coordinate assignments independently and uniformly. The complementary arm draws one uniformly and complements every free bit for the second history, completing the dependent bits within the same public affine constraint. Each member remains uniform over the same feasible posterior. Therefore pair-averaged loss and mean gradient have the same expectation in both arms. Their covariance may differ; no general variance-reduction guarantee is claimed. First-round inputs are identical within each pair; their duplicated compute is retained in both arms to match the protocol.

Each 8-GPU update contains 16 public conditions (8 per family), with 2 histories each, for 32 serial document forwards. Both arms share public conditions, reveal rounds, first histories, native token counts, AdamW optimizer, LR3e-5, FP32 tensor/default Triton precision, and initialization. The second history coupling is the only arm difference. New training indices begin at 48000. N8 remains the original terminal task throughout.

Qualification: 20 updates per arm, discarded. Verify finite full-model gradients, serialized-document consistency, no cross-document leakage, checkpoint loading, and parent identity. Research restarts from the original shared step1500 checkpoint. Fixed research checkpoints after 400 and 1000 additional updates; preserve both arms through the full endpoint. Exact development audits compare original policies and revealed-history response. No confirmation-panel access or checkpoint selection on test results. A positive result requires useful absolute parity fidelity and history response, not just a small relative KL difference between failed predictors.

GPU cap: 32 concurrent queued/running H100s, at most8/job; shared submission lock and scheduler-cap checks. No jobs are yet launched by this plan document.

# Claim boundaries for the independent header/task-position study

The frozen analysis is in `report.json`; the complete tables retain all six checkpoints and all six layouts. All six selectors are validated. The primary error reduction is 7.626609 nats, conditional 95% interval [6.908102, 8.301452], with all three positive directions. All predeclared criteria pass. The largest absolute mean co-located-versus-legacy discrepancy is 4.089890 nats; header effects and distractor-order sensitivity rule out layout invariance.

- The intervention separates the positions of a generic instruction header and a task-bearing block at inference. It does not separate the components of the earlier training intervention.
- The task block contains equation structure, coordinate labels and output-order metadata. The experiment cannot isolate factual retention from binding and readout.
- Moving blocks preserves token content and length, but changes sequence order. Co-located and legacy controls quantify the effect of regrouping irrelevant records; these layouts are not token-identical.
- The target dependence term remains zero for the information-set policy. Layout effects therefore enter conditional prediction error. This follows from the established KL decomposition; it is not a new general theorem about memory.
- The predeclared stronger interpretation requires a positive primary interval, all three positive seed effects, and every balanced model below the one-call product floor in both nearby-header cells. Positive arm differences alone are insufficient.
- The 32 public prompts are fresh relative to the preceding panel, but use the same previously observed structure catalog. All six adaptations share one selected starting checkpoint. Problem-bootstrap intervals condition on those checkpoints and that lineage.
- This study adds no training, architecture comparison, latency measurement, natural-language evidence, or official-kernel parity validation. The earlier restricted empirical cost certificate is unchanged.
- The earlier exhaustive counterfactual study's rare collateral errors remain in the abstract, main text and appendix. A positive layout result does not establish uniform selectivity.
- One evaluation attempt stopped in a scheduler startup/queue-migration race before recorded predictions. Its scheduler reports zero running time despite startup logs. A manually reviewed replacement retains the frozen checkpoint, source, inputs and analysis in a distinct output directory. Both attempts are retained in resource accounting; this was not an automatic retry.

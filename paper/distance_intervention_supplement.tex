Each main adaptation processes 315,283,200 native input tokens over 600 updates;
the six matched runs therefore process 1,891,699,200 tokens.
The discarded six-update qualification adds 3,152,832 tokens. Scheduler
accounting for this follow-up totals 106.34 H100-hours, including qualification,
all seven evaluations, the matched attention timing job, and three failed
launches before model loading. It excludes the earlier checkpoint-training
history described in the main text and preceding studies.
The failed launches used a project-local execution-manifest path that was
unavailable on workers; the repair changed storage location without changing
manifest bytes, training source, or selectors. All failed-launch receipts
remain in the accounting. No GPU job was automatically retried.

Authoritative created-to-finished scheduler intervals, including queue
reservations, show a peak of 48 H100s: at most 32 in the primary project and 16
in the additional project, with at most eight GPUs per job.
All 18 study jobs are terminal and the final campaign census is zero.
Main-run steady GPU-busy measurements range from 95.6\% to 96.3\%, while memory
occupancy is approximately 25.9\%. GPU busy is not achieved FLOP utilization.
Memory occupancy did not reach 80\%; fixed experiments followed the authorized
priority of fastest completion without changing the scientific protocol.

training/evaluation manifests. The collectors validate raw endpoints,

regenerated with the report, figures, and cost-certificate scripts under
